01 / THE WMFORGE STORY

Less theory.
More practical understanding.

WMForge is a practical learning space for people who want to understand how SAP Warehouse Management works beyond definitions and screenshots.

We break warehouse processes into clear steps—from inbound delivery and putaway to picking, transfer orders, goods issue, and configuration. Our goal is to make complex topics easier to follow with demonstrations, reference material, and real-world-style scenarios.
