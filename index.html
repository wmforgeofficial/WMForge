Built for people who want to understand SAP WM in practice.

Not just what to do in SAP — but why each step matters.

WMForge is an independent learning space focused on practical SAP Warehouse Management. We explain warehouse processes in clear, manageable steps, from inbound activities and putaway to picking, transfer orders, goods issue, and configuration.

Our aim is to make technical topics easier to follow through demonstrations, process references, and hands-on style examples you can revisit as you practise.
